Mobility routine
3× per week · ~25 min · same every time
Block 1 Stability ~6 min
Hip Abduction Isometrics
2 × 20 sec · 2 min rest
Stand sideways to wall, press outer knee isometrically into wall. Hold 20 sec, switch sides.
Stability
Book Openings
2 × 8 reps / side
Lie on your side, knees bent level with hips, arms stacked out front. Inhale and float the top arm to the ceiling; exhale, turn your head to look behind, letting chest and arm open backwards, one vertebra at a time. Inhale hold, exhale return. Slow and controlled.
Stability
Glute Bridges
2 × 12 reps
On your back, knees bent, feet flat. Drive through the heels, lift hips until shoulders–hips–knees form a line. Squeeze the glutes at the top, lower with control. Don't arch the low back.
Strength
Block 2 Control + Power ~9 min
Single Leg Airplane
2 × 8 reps / side · 1 min rest
Stand on one leg, slight knee bend. Tip forward like a T, back leg rises. Nice and slow. 1 min rest between sets.
Stability
Front Step Down
2 × 12 reps / side
Stand on step, slowly lower one foot to floor in front. Control the descent. No step? Just step forward to the ground.
Strength
Counter Movement Jumps
2 × 6 reps · 1 min rest
Quick dip then explosive jump — max effort. Soft landing, absorb through hips and knees. Full 1 min between sets.
Power
Block 3 Strength + Plyometrics ~5 min
Jump Lunges
2 × 8 reps / side
Lunge position, jump and switch legs in the air. Land softly back in lunge. Explosive but controlled.
Power
Dead Bug
2 × 10 reps / side
Lie on back, arms and legs up. Lower opposite arm and leg slowly towards floor. Lower back stays pressed flat. Slow and deliberate.
Stability
Sprinter Heel Raises
2 × 15 reps / side
Stand tall, one knee raised in sprint position. Rise onto the toes of the standing leg, controlled return.
Strength
Block 4 Planks ~3 min
Front Plank
2 × 30 sec
Forearms on ground, body straight from head to heels. Don't let hips sag or pike up. Breathe.
Stability
Short Side Plank
2 × 20 sec / side
From your knees, on one forearm. Knees push into the ground; lift and hold the top leg up — no movement, just an isometric hold. Switch sides.
Stability
Block 5 Stretches ~5 min · hold each 40–50 sec
Standing Quad Stretch
40–50 sec / side
Stand tall, pull one foot towards glute. Keep knees together. Balance hand on wall if needed.
Stretch
Kneeling Hip Flexor
40–50 sec / side
Back knee on floor, front foot forward. Drive hips gently forward. Feel it in the front of the back hip. Upright torso.
Stretch
Thoracic Rotation
8 slow reps / side
All fours. One hand behind head. Rotate that elbow towards the ceiling, opening the chest. Follow with eyes. Slow.
Stretch
Child's Pose with Side Reach
40–50 sec, then 20 sec / side
Kneel, sit hips back to heels, arms reaching long in front, forehead toward the floor — let the whole back lengthen. Then walk both hands right for 20 sec, then left, to open each side. Breathe into it.
Stretch
~25 min total · do this on Wed and Sat as scheduled, and any third day that works.
The first few weeks will feel long — it gets faster as the movements become automatic.

Heart-rate zones
Provisional · HRmax est. 188 · confirm with a 20-min LTHR test at W13
Z1 Recovery · 113–132 bpm
Shakeouts, warm-up, cooldown. Indicative 5:50–6:15/km. Easy enough to feel almost pointless.
Z2 Easy / Aerobic · 132–150 bpm
Easy + long runs. Indicative 5:25–5:45/km. Cap at 150 — this is where the engine is built. Full conversation.
Z3 Tempo · 150–163 bpm
Tempo / marathon-pace work. Indicative 4:45–4:55/km. Short sentences only.
Z4 Threshold · 163–173 bpm
Threshold reps. Indicative 4:20–4:28/km. Comfortably hard — words, not sentences.
Z5 VO₂max · 173–188 bpm
Intervals / reps. Indicative 3:55–4:10/km. Pace leads here — HR lags on short reps, don't chase it.
Race 1:30 goal pace · 4:16/km
HR drifts Z3→Z4 over the race (≈150 early, ≈175 late). Pace is the boss; let HR rise.
Run easy by heart rate, run hard by pace. Zones are provisional until you field-test — then they lock.
